This website exists to provide comprehensive, low fluff guides for all pokemon games. As this is a massive project and I'm only one man, many guides are currently unfinished or not even started. If you notice any errors or want to suggest an improvement to the site, please contact me here:

Why use these guides over Serebii/Bulbapedia/etc.?

While sites like Serebii and Bulbapedia are great resources for game data and how mechanics work (I actually use and link to both very often), they don't always make for great user guides. The goal of this page isn't just to provide information of how shiny hunting works, but also to make translating that into real in-game hunts easier for the average person. Video guides are also more likely to contain misinformation since they cannot be updated after the initial upload.
