SM/USUM Shiny Hunting Guide

Method 1 - Masuda

Introduced in Gen 4, the Masuda method is one of the most straight-forward and reliable methods to shiny hunt Pokémon. When you breed two Pokémon of different nationalities, the baby has an increased chance of being shiny. In gen 7, the odds of finding a shiny Pokémon using the Masuda method are 1/682.7 without the shiny charm and 1/512 with the shiny charm.

Setup

You will need:

  • Two Pokémon in compatible egg groups with different nationalities (ditto works well for this.)
  • The Shiny Charm (Optional, increases shiny odds.)
  • The Oval Charm (Optional, makes your Pokémon produce eggs faster.)
  • A Pokémon with Flame Body or Magma Armor (Optional, makes eggs hatch faster when one is in your party.)
  • The Destiny Knot (Optional, passes down 5 IVs from the parent Pokémon at random.)
  • The Everstone (Optional, will pass down the nature from whichever parent is holding it.)

For more information on Pokémon breeding check out this article on Serebii.

As of April 8th, 2024, the in-game online trading system has shut down. To get Pokémon with different nationalities in gen 7 you will now either need:

  1. Two 3DS/2DS consoles & Two Gen 7 games (set to different languages) to trade between.
  2. A friend to trade you the Pokémon locally.

Breeding and Hatching the Eggs

Now for the easy part. Simply hand over your Pokémon to the lady inside the daycare and talk to the lady outside when she crosses her arms to get your eggs. The exact method you use to get and hatch eggs mostly comes down to personal preference. Some people like to hatch the eggs as they are given to them, and some people like to stockpile a few boxes of eggs and hatch them all at once. Regardless of your method, you should end up with a shiny Pokémon after a while. The fastest method of hatching eggs is to run in circles on Tauros in the pen directly below the daycare. Method 2 - S.O.S Calling

This method is not 100% consistent between SM and USUM. The shiny rates are worse in base SM than in USUM and SOS chains reset at 255 in SM.

Introduction

S.O.S. calling is the mechanic where wild Pokémon call for another support Pokémon in battle. The more times this happens in a single battle, the more often rare properties will apply to the called-in Pokémon, including shininess.

In SM, the odds of finding a shiny Pokémon increase to 1/1024 (1/683 with Shiny Charm) after 70 SOS calls. In USUM, the odds of finding a shiny increase to 1/315 (1/273 w/ Shiny Charm) after 30 SOS calls.

Setup

You will need:

  • A Catching Pokémon with a KO move
  • Ethers, Leppa Berries, or Rotom PP Restore
  • An Exeggutor with Harvest, Skill Swap, and Bestow holding a Leppa berry. (Optional, makes hunts harder to fail.)
  • An intimidate pokemon (Optional, increases S.O.S. call chance.)
  • Adrenaline Orbs (Optional, increases S.O.S. call chance.)

Most wild Pokémon can be hunted using S.O.S. but some Pokémon are particularly difficult due to having moves that damage or KO themselves. If the Pokémon you want to hunt has one of these moves, I encourage you to bring a move/ability that nullifies it, find a different location where it doesn't know that move in the wild, hunt a pre-evolution of the Pokémon that doesn't know the move, or hunt it using a different method.

Chaining S.O.S. Calls

To setup the wild Pokémon follow these steps:

  1. Encounter your target Pokémon and choose your lead Pokémon in this order depending on what you brought:
    1. Intimidate Pokémon
    2. Exeggutor
    3. Catching/KO Pokémon
  2. If you brought the Exeggutor setup skill swap and bestow.
  3. Use your catching Pokémon to lower it's health to 1 with false swipe.
  4. Use an Adrenaline Orb if you have one.

After setting up, KO all Pokémon that are called in until a shiny shows up. If you didn't bring Exeggutor make sure to replace the calling Pokémon every once in a while so it never struggles and faints itself.

Method 3 - Ultra Wormholes

This method is only available in USUM.

USUM introduced a mechanic called "ultra wormholes". In the post-game, if you visit the Altar of the Sunne/Moone on Poni island, you can interact with a warp hole to enter Ultra Space. During this, you will play a mini-game that involves you riding on your game's Box Legendary past wormholes of different colors and with rings of increasing intensity around them. When you enter a wormhole, you'll find yourself in a new area with a path leading to a Pokémon. The Pokémon you get is determined by the color of the wormhole and the border it has. White wormholes will always lead you to an ultra beast, while colored wormholes will lead you to a random standard or legendary Pokémon chosen from a limited pool based on the specific color. The further into Ultra Space you fly, the more likely you are to see wormholes with higher intensity rings. The higher the rings intensity, the more likely you are to find a legendary Pokémon. If you find a legendary, your screen will flash white shortly before you regain control of your character. For a full list of which Pokémon appear in which wormholes and which Pokémon are version exclusives, refer to this Serebii page.

As shininess is determined differently for standard and legendary Pokémon in Ultra Wormholes, each hunting method will be covered separately below.

Setup

You will need:

  • Access to the warp hole at the altar
  • A (High Level) Catching Pokémon
  • An Exeggutor with Harvest, Skill Swap, and Bestow holding a Leppa Berry (Optional)

You will also probably want to switch the default motion controls to use the circle pad. You can do this at the Game Freak building in Heahea City.

Hunting Legendaries

This is about as simple as a shiny hunt can get. Simply fly into different wormholes until you find the legendary you want to hunt. Save before you encounter it, and soft reset the game until it shines. If you enter a wormhole and it isn't the legendary you are looking for, run up and check if it's shiny anyway. Many people have gotten random shinies doing just that. Just make sure you don't catch a legendary you intend to hunt in the future if it isn't shiny as you can only catch each legendary once.

The odds for legendaries are 1/4096 without the shiny charm and 1/1365.3 with it.

Hunting Regular Pokemon

This is also pretty straight-forward. The shininess of a non-legendary Pokémon is determined the moment you enter the wormhole. So all you have to do is enter wormholes, check if the Pokémon is shiny, and leave if it isn't.
