Pokemon Terminology

This page contains common terminology used within the pokémon community.

General Terms (WIP)

Term Meaning
IVs Individual Values - Inherent attributes assaigned to each pokemon that affect how proficient they are in each stat. There is one per stat ranging from 0 to 31. The higher the IV for a stat the higher the stat can go. Introduced in Gen 3.
DVs Determinant Values - The Gens 1 & 2 equivelant to IVs. 0 - 15.
IV/DV Spread The distribution of IVs or DVs on a pokemon.

Shiny Terms (WIP)

Term Meaning
MM/Masuda Method When two pokémon of different nationalities breed for increased shiny odds
CF/Chain Fishing A shiny hunting method in which shiny odds increase for every consecutive pokémon reeled in with a fishing rod
BQ/Badge Quest A challenge run in which the player must catch a shiny pokémon before every gym battle sometimes including the elite four
DTQ/Dream Team Quest A challenge run in which the player chooses multiple pokémon (usually 6 for a full team) they must hunt as soon as they become available
