Introduction to Shiny Pokemon

What Are Shiny Pokémon?

Shiny pokemon are rare form of pokemon with a different coloration to the norm for that species. They were first introduced in generation 2 (Gold/Silver/Crystal) and have been a staple of the series ever since. Shinies are also extremely rare only appearing about once per every 8192 pokemon before gen 6 and once per 4096 pokemon in newer games, making them very valuble to many.

If Shinies are that rare how come my friends have so many?

The odds listed above are only the base odds. There are several methods to increase your chances of finding shiny pokemon. Take the masuda method for example, which in combination with the shiny charm can increase your odds all the way to 1/512 from 1/4096 in newer (Gen 6+) games. These methods are what most of the guides on this website are about.

Erm Ackchyually...
Most of these methods don't directly influence shiny odds. Instead they provide 're-rolls' which gives the pokemon another oportunity to be shiny. For example the shiny charm gives +2 rolls effectively increasing odds from 1/4096 to 3/4096 by rolling 1/4096 3 times and marking the pokemon shiny if **any** of the rolls would result in a shiny.
